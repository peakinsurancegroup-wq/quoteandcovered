Privacy & your choices

Last updated October 2026. This site is operated by Peak Insurance Group LLC, the independently owned and operated GEICO Local Office of John Sanchez in Denver, Colorado.

What this site does

quoteandcovered.com has one job: it takes the ZIP code you enter and sends you to GEICO's secure quote page with our office attached. We do not store your ZIP code, and we never ask for your name, email, or phone number on this site. Everything after the handoff happens on GEICO's website under GEICO's privacy policy.

Advertising measurement

We use Google Ads conversion tracking and remarketing tags. They let us know that a visitor who clicked one of our ads started a quote or tapped our phone number, and they may be used to show you our ads again on Google services. These tags set cookies in your browser. They do not receive your ZIP code or any information you enter on GEICO's site.

Your choices, including Colorado residents

Under the Colorado Privacy Act you may opt out of the processing of your personal data for targeted advertising. You can do that in any of these ways:

  • Turn off ad personalization in your Google account at adssettings.google.com.
  • Use a browser or device setting that sends an opt-out preference signal, which we honor.
  • Email John@sierrainsurance.co or call 303-824-3430 and ask to opt out. We respond within 45 days as the law requires.

We do not sell personal data.
